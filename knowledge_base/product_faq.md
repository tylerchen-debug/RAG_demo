# AI Pack LLC — Product FAQ

Welcome to AI Pack! We're a platform that lets you design one-of-a-kind custom products with the help of AI. Whether you're making a birthday gift, launching merch for your small business, or just creating something for yourself, this page answers the questions we hear most often.

## What exactly does AI Pack do?

AI Pack turns your ideas into real, physical products. You describe what you want in your own words, our AI generates four designs, and once you've picked the one you love, we print and ship the finished item to you. Right now our most popular products are custom apparel like t-shirts and hoodies, tote bags, mugs, phone cases, stickers, and framed prints. We add new product types regularly, so it's worth checking back if you don't see what you're looking for.

## How do I create a design?

You start by typing a prompt — basically a description of what you'd like. For example, "a watercolor fox wearing a scarf, soft autumn colors" or "a retro 80s sunset with the words Stay Weird." The AI will generate four options for you to choose from. From there you can keep refining: ask for a different color palette, a cleaner background, a different art style, or tweak the text. Most people go through three or four rounds before they land on something they love, and there's no charge for generating or refining designs. You only pay when you order a physical product.

## Can I upload my own image instead of using a prompt?

Not at the moment. AI Pack creates designs from your written prompts rather than uploaded files, so everything starts with a description of what you'd like. If you're picturing a specific logo or photo, describe it in words and the AI will do its best to bring it to life. For precise or professional work, our human design assist service can help.

## How long does it take to get my design ready?

The AI usually generates designs in under a minute. The design stage is entirely up to you — some people finish in five minutes, others spend an afternoon experimenting. Once you place an order, production and shipping timelines kick in, which are covered in our Shipping Policy.

## Do I own the designs I create?

When you place a paid order, you receive full commercial rights to that specific design, meaning you can use it for personal or business purposes, including reselling products that feature it. Designs you generate but never order remain in a preview state and aren't licensed for commercial use. There are a few important nuances around AI-generated content and copyright, so please read the AI Generation Policy for the complete picture.

## Can I use my designs for my own business or merch line?

Absolutely — a lot of our customers are small business owners and creators. Once you've paid for an order, the design is yours to sell on. If you're planning a larger run or want consistent restocks, reach out to our team about bulk and wholesale options; we can often offer better per-unit pricing.

## What if I don't like any of the AI's suggestions?

That's completely normal, especially early on. The trick is usually in the prompt — being more specific about style, mood, and colors tends to produce much better results. If you're stuck, our Design Guidelines document has tips and example prompts. You can also start over as many times as you'd like at no cost.

## Can someone from your team help me with a design?

Yes. If you'd rather have a human involved, we offer a design assist service where one of our designers helps refine your concept. This is a paid add-on for more complex or professional projects. Just contact support and describe what you're going for.

## How much do products cost?

Pricing depends on the product type, size, and quantity. Each item shows its price before you add it to your cart, and the full total including shipping and any applicable taxes appears at checkout. Generating and refining designs is always free — you're only charged for physical products you order.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, as well as Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. For large or wholesale orders we can also arrange invoicing — contact us to set that up.

## Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Because each item is made specifically for you, there's a short window after ordering during which changes or cancellations are possible. After production begins, we usually can't stop or alter an order. Details and exact timing are in our Refund Policy.

## What happens if my product arrives damaged or looks wrong?

We stand behind our work. If your item arrives damaged, defective, or noticeably different from the design you approved, contact us with a photo and we'll make it right — usually with a free reprint or a refund. Our Refund Policy explains exactly what qualifies and how to submit a claim.

## Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to most countries. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination, and international orders may be subject to customs fees. See the Shipping Policy for specifics.

## Is my data and my designs kept private?

We take privacy seriously. Your prompts and designs are tied to your account and aren't shared publicly unless you choose to make them public in a community gallery. We don't sell your personal data. For more on how AI-generated content is stored and used, see the AI Generation Policy.

## How do I contact support?

The fastest way is through the in-app chat or by emailing support@aipack.com. We typically respond within one business day. If your question is about an existing order, having your order number handy speeds things up.

## Still have a question?

If something isn't covered here, just reach out — we're a small, friendly team and we genuinely like hearing what people are making. Chances are your question will help us improve this page for the next person.
