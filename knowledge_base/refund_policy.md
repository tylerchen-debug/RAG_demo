# AI Pack LLC — Refund Policy

We want you to be genuinely happy with what you make on AI Pack. At the same time, nearly everything we sell is custom-made to your specifications, which shapes how refunds and cancellations work. This page explains, in plain language, when you can get a refund, when you can't, and how to reach out if something's gone wrong. If anything here is unclear, contact us at support@aipack.com and we'll walk you through it.

## The short version

Because each item is printed on demand just for you, we generally can't accept returns or offer refunds for reasons like changing your mind, ordering the wrong size, or deciding you don't like the design after it's made. However, if there's a problem that's our fault — a defect, a printing error, damage in transit, or an item that doesn't match the design you approved — we'll absolutely make it right, usually with a free reprint or a full refund. Your satisfaction with our quality is something we stand behind completely.

## Changing or canceling an order

Since production can begin quickly after you check out, there's only a short window during which we can change or cancel an order. If you contact us within 2 hours of placing your order, we can usually cancel it for a full refund or make edits, as long as it hasn't entered production yet. After production has started, the materials and printing are already committed, so we're typically unable to cancel, change the design, or adjust the size or quantity. If you catch a mistake, reach out immediately — the sooner you contact us, the more likely we can help.

## When you're entitled to a refund or reprint

We'll provide a free replacement or a full refund if your order arrives with any of the following problems:

Your item is defective or has a manufacturing flaw, such as peeling print, crooked placement well outside normal tolerance, stitching defects, or a broken product. Your item arrived damaged during shipping. The item you received doesn't match the design you approved — for example, the wrong colors, the wrong artwork, or the wrong product entirely. You received the wrong item or the wrong size compared to what your order confirmation shows.

In these cases the mistake is on our side, and we don't expect you to pay for it. We'll either reprint and reship the correct item at no cost to you or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## When we generally can't offer a refund

Because these are custom products, the following situations usually aren't eligible for a refund:

You changed your mind after the item was produced. You ordered the wrong size, color, or product by mistake (please double-check your selections and our size charts before ordering). There's a typo or spelling error that was present in the design you approved — the printer faithfully reproduces the design you signed off on, so please proofread carefully. The design looks slightly different in person than it did on screen due to normal variation in monitors, garment colors, and printing.

None of these mean we won't talk to you — we review situations case by case and we're reasonable people. But as a baseline, custom items made correctly to your approved specs aren't returnable.

## Color, size, and print variation

A quick note on what counts as "normal." Slight differences in color between your screen and the printed product are expected — screens are backlit and every monitor is calibrated a little differently, so a shade of blue may look marginally different in person. Print placement can also vary by a small margin from item to item. These minor variations are a normal part of custom printing and aren't considered defects. That said, if the difference is dramatic and clearly wrong, that's a quality issue and we want to hear about it.

## How to submit a claim

If something's wrong with your order, here's what to do. Contact us within 30 days of receiving your item, either through the in-app chat or by emailing support@aipack.com. Include your order number and one or two clear photos showing the problem. Photos help us diagnose the issue quickly and, when needed, follow up with our print partners to prevent it happening again.

Once we receive your claim, we'll review it — usually within one to two business days — and let you know the next steps. If it's a defect or an error on our end, we'll arrange a free reprint or a refund right away. You typically won't need to ship the faulty item back, but occasionally we may ask for it or ask for additional photos to help us investigate.

## How refunds are issued

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Once we process the refund on our side, it usually takes 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer. If you paid with PayPal or a digital wallet, refunds are often faster. We'll email you a confirmation when the refund is on its way.

## Digital designs and design fees

Generating and refining designs on AI Pack is free, so there's nothing to refund there. If you purchased a paid add-on such as our human design assist service, that work is performed by our team specifically for you, so it's generally non-refundable once the work has been delivered. If you're unhappy with a paid design service, talk to us — we'd rather keep working with you until you're satisfied than leave you disappointed.

## Bulk and wholesale orders

Large or wholesale orders may be covered by a separate agreement with specific terms around cancellation, defects, and returns. If you placed a bulk order, please refer to your order agreement or contact your account representative. Where a bulk agreement is silent, this policy applies.

## Lost or delayed shipments

If your order never arrives or is significantly delayed, that's usually a shipping matter rather than a product defect, but we'll still help. Please see our Shipping Policy for how we handle lost, stuck, or delayed packages, and reach out to support so we can track it down and, if it's genuinely lost, reship or refund it.

## Our promise

At the end of the day, we're a small company that cares about the things people make with us. We can't take back a custom item just because someone changed their mind, but we will never leave you stuck with a product that's defective or wrong because of us. If you're ever unsure whether your situation qualifies, just ask — we'd rather have the conversation than lose your trust over a policy technicality.
