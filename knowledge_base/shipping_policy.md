# AI Pack LLC — Shipping Policy

Once you've created something you love and placed your order, the next thing on your mind is naturally "when will it get here?" This page explains how shipping works at AI Pack — how long production takes, what delivery options we offer, where we ship, and what happens if a package goes astray. Because our products are made to order, shipping timelines have two parts: the time it takes to make your item, and the time it takes to deliver it.

## Production time comes first

Everything we sell is printed on demand, which means we don't have finished products sitting on a shelf waiting to go out. When you order, we make your item specifically for you. Production typically takes 2 to 5 business days depending on the product and how busy we are. Apparel and simpler items are usually on the faster end; framed prints and more complex products can take a little longer. During peak seasons like the winter holidays, production can take a bit more time, and we'll note any expected delays at checkout.

Production time is separate from shipping time. So if production takes 3 business days and shipping takes 5, your total time from order to doorstep is roughly 8 business days. We'll always show you an estimated delivery window before you check out.

## Shipping options and timing

Once your item is produced, it ships via one of our carrier partners. We offer a few speeds:

Standard shipping usually arrives within 3 to 7 business days after production, and it's our most economical option. Expedited shipping usually arrives within 2 to 3 business days after production. Express shipping is the fastest we offer, typically 1 to 2 business days after production, for when you're in a hurry.

Keep in mind these windows describe transit time after your item has been made — they don't shorten production time. If you need something by a specific date, choosing a faster shipping speed helps, but the safest bet is to order with a comfortable buffer.

## Shipping costs

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your destination, the shipping speed you choose, and the size and weight of your order. You'll always see the exact shipping cost before you pay — there are no surprise fees added later. From time to time we run free standard shipping promotions or offer free shipping over a certain order total; any active offer will show up automatically in your cart.

## Where we ship

We ship across the United States, including Alaska, Hawaii, and most U.S. territories, and we ship internationally to most countries. Domestic orders are straightforward. For international orders, please read the next section carefully, since customs and delivery times work differently across borders.

## International orders, customs, and duties

We're happy to ship worldwide, but international shipping comes with a few things to know. Delivery times vary quite a bit by destination and can range from about 7 to 21 business days after production, sometimes longer for remote regions or during customs backlogs. International packages may be subject to import duties, taxes, or customs fees charged by the destination country. These fees are set by your local government, not by us, and they're the responsibility of the recipient. We have no way to calculate or collect them in advance, so please check your country's import rules if you're concerned about extra costs. Occasionally customs can hold a package for inspection, which adds delay and is outside our control.

## Tracking your order

As soon as your item ships, we'll email you a tracking number and a link so you can follow its journey. You can also find tracking details in your account under your order history. If tracking hasn't updated in a few days, don't panic — carrier scans sometimes lag, especially over weekends and holidays. If it's been stuck with no movement for a while, reach out and we'll investigate.

## Wrong address and failed deliveries

Please double-check your shipping address before you place your order. Because production starts quickly, we often can't update the address after the fact, and we can't be responsible for packages sent to an address you entered incorrectly. If a package is returned to us because of a wrong or incomplete address, we can usually reship it, though you may need to cover the additional shipping cost. If you realize you made an address mistake, contact us immediately at support@aipack.com and we'll do our best to catch it before it ships.

## Delayed packages

Sometimes packages move slower than expected due to weather, carrier backlogs, or customs. While we can't control what happens once a package leaves our facility, we're here to help you track it down. If your order is running well past its estimated delivery window, get in touch and we'll open an inquiry with the carrier. Most delayed packages do eventually arrive; for the rare ones that don't, see the next section.

## Lost packages

If tracking shows your package as delivered but you can't find it, start by checking around your property, with neighbors, and with anyone else at your address, and give it a day — carriers occasionally mark items delivered a little early. If it still doesn't turn up, contact us. For packages that are genuinely lost in transit and never arrive, we'll work with the carrier and, once it's confirmed lost, reship your order or issue a refund. We won't leave you empty-handed for something that got lost on the way.

## Multiple items in one order

If you order several items, they may not all ship together. Because different products can have different production times, we sometimes split an order into multiple shipments so the faster items don't wait on the slower ones. If your order ships in parts, you'll receive separate tracking numbers, and you won't be charged extra for the split.

## Damaged in transit

If your package arrives looking beat up and the product inside is damaged, that's covered under our Refund Policy. Take a couple of photos of the packaging and the item, then contact support and we'll arrange a free reprint or a refund. Please report shipping damage promptly — within 30 days of delivery — so we can act on it quickly.

## Questions about your shipment

If you're ever unsure where your order is or when it'll arrive, just reach out through in-app chat or email support@aipack.com with your order number. We'd rather you ask than sit and wonder. We're a small team and we genuinely want your order to arrive safely and on time.
