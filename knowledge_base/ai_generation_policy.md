# AI Pack LLC — AI Generation Policy

AI is at the heart of what we do at AI Pack. It's what lets you turn a few words into a real, custom product. Because AI-generated content raises some genuine questions around ownership, acceptable use, and privacy, we've put together this policy to explain how it all works in plain language. Our goal is to keep things fair, safe, and transparent — for you, for other creators, and for us. By using AI Pack to generate designs, you agree to the terms described here.

## How our AI works, in brief

When you type a prompt, our system uses AI models to generate original artwork based on your input. The AI doesn't copy existing images and paste them together; it produces new visuals based on patterns it has learned. That said, no AI is perfect, and it's possible for a generated design to unintentionally resemble existing work. That's part of why the rules below exist — to keep everyone on the right side of both good taste and the law.

## Who owns the designs you create

Let's talk about ownership, since it's the question we get most. When you place a paid order for a product featuring a design, we grant you a broad license to use that specific design for personal and commercial purposes — including selling products that feature it. In everyday terms, the design is yours to use and profit from.

There's an important legal nuance worth understanding, though. In the United States and several other countries, purely AI-generated images may not be eligible for copyright protection the same way human-authored works are, because current law generally requires meaningful human authorship for a copyright to be granted. What this means in practice is that while you're free to use and sell your design, you may not be able to stop someone else from using a similar AI-generated image, and you shouldn't assume you hold an enforceable copyright over a design that was generated entirely by the AI without significant human creative input. The more you shape, edit, and add your own creative decisions to a design, the stronger your position tends to be. We're not your lawyers, and this isn't legal advice — if commercial rights are critical to your business, it's worth consulting an attorney about your specific situation.

Designs you generate but never order stay in a preview state. They're not licensed for commercial use, and we may not retain them indefinitely.

## What you're responsible for

You're responsible for the prompts you write and how you use the designs you create. The words you choose shape what the AI produces, so please don't prompt it to recreate content you don't have the right to use.

Similarly, you're responsible for making sure the designs you create and sell don't infringe on anyone else's rights. If you ask the AI to reproduce a famous logo or character and then sell it, the legal risk is yours, not ours. When in doubt, keep it original.

## What's not allowed

To keep AI Pack safe and lawful, certain content is off-limits. You may not use our platform to create designs that:

Infringe on someone else's intellectual property — this includes copyrighted characters, brand logos, trademarked slogans, and deliberately copying another artist's distinctive style for commercial gain. Depict recognizable real people without their consent, including celebrities, in a way that misuses their likeness. Contain hateful, harassing, or discriminatory content targeting people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar characteristics. Include sexually explicit material, especially anything involving minors, which we report to authorities as required by law. Promote violence, terrorism, self-harm, or illegal activity. Spread deliberate misinformation designed to deceive, or impersonate real individuals or organizations. Are otherwise unlawful in the jurisdiction where the product will be sold or shipped.

We use a combination of automated filters and human review to catch content that violates these rules. If a design is flagged, we may decline to produce it, cancel the order, and in serious or repeated cases, suspend the account involved.

## Prohibited prompts and workarounds

Trying to trick the system into producing banned content — for example, by rephrasing a prompt to sneak past our filters — is itself a violation of this policy. Our filters aren't perfect, and getting something past them doesn't make it allowed. If you're unsure whether an idea crosses a line, err on the side of caution or ask us first.

## Content moderation and order rejection

We reserve the right to review, decline, or cancel any order that we believe violates this policy or the law, even after it's been placed. If we cancel an order for a policy violation, we'll refund any charge for the physical product. We aim to be fair and consistent, and if you think a design was flagged in error, you can contact support and we'll take another look. There are gray areas in creative work, and we'd rather have a conversation than shut something down unfairly.

## How we handle your prompts and designs

Your prompts and generated designs are tied to your account. We store them so you can return to your work, reorder, and manage your projects. We don't sell your personal data, and we don't publish your designs publicly unless you choose to share them — for example, by opting into a community gallery.

We may use aggregated, de-identified information about how people use the platform to improve our service and our AI models — things like which styles are popular or where the AI tends to struggle. Where we use content to improve our systems, we work to do so in a way that respects your privacy and doesn't expose your personal designs to others. If you'd like to know more about how your data is handled or want your data removed, contact us at support@aipack.com and we'll help.

## AI limitations and accuracy

AI-generated art is impressive but imperfect. It can misspell text, distort hands and faces, and occasionally produce odd or unexpected results. It has no understanding of real-world facts, trademarks, or whether something is offensive — that judgment is on you and, as a backstop, on our review process. Please review your design carefully before ordering, since what you approve is what we print. We can't be responsible for quirks or errors in an AI-generated design that you reviewed and approved.

## Changes to this policy

As AI technology and the laws around it continue to evolve, we may update this policy from time to time. When we make meaningful changes, we'll do our best to let you know. Continuing to use AI Pack after an update means you accept the revised policy.

If you have a question about what you can create, what you can sell, or how your data is handled, please reach out to support@aipack.com. We'd much rather help you understand than have you guess.
